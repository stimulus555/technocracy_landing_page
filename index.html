03 / VIGYAAN · OUR JOURNEY

FROM VIGYAAN TO AAVARTAN.

Vigyaan, a national-level science exhibition, began in 2007. In 2011 it evolved into Aavartan; by 2019, Aavartan welcomed over 10,000 visitors and became Central India’s largest tech-fest.
